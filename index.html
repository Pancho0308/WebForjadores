EIGHT DAYS TO COME TOGETHER

A celebration full of life

Here's what we're preparing for Día de Muertos. Activities are in development; the final program and times are still to be confirmed.

01 /

Opening & closing ceremonies

A candle-lit welcome, a community photo and a closing celebration with a cempasúchil bridge, music and light.

02 /

Music, dance & procession

Gatherings with dancers and musicians, and a procession to share the spirit of the celebration with other communities.

03 /

Stories & cultural tours

Guided tours, storytelling and spaces to discover the history of Día de Muertos and the meaning of the ofrenda.

04 /

An ofrenda we build together

Candles, remembrance messages and interactive objects: a shared tribute to those we carry in our hearts.

05 /

Explore, play & discover

Collectibles, cultural quizzes, ofrenda puzzles and alebrijes to discover as you explore the world.

06 /

Flame of the Souls

A milestone experience connecting activities to the ofrenda's progression, alongside art and gallery spaces.
