EIGHT DAYS TO COME TOGETHER

A celebration full of life

Discover the activities of our VRChat celebration. Published times are in EST; dates and times not listed are to be confirmed. Throughout the week, explore the world, visit cultural locations and complete your ofrenda at your own pace.

01 /

HorrorCon Handoff & Opening Ceremony

Opening · 10:30 PM EST

As HorrorCon ends, its portal will welcome visitors to our celebration. The world will already be open before the transition, and hosts will introduce the daily collectibles, personal ofrenda, cultural locations and The Flame of the Souls. Set 1 will be available as you begin exploring.

02 /

Journey to Mictlán / Cultural Guides

November 2 · 7:00–10:00 PM EST

Explore seven interactive locations: La Ofrenda, Camino de Cempasúchil, El Panteón, La Mesa de Comida, El Agua, Papel Picado y Celebración, and Mictlán. Learn, ask questions and talk with the guides, with no required route and language-based groups when possible. Collectible set 2 unlocks tonight.

03 /

Performance Nights

November 3, 4 & 6 · Times to be confirmed

The Stand becomes a stage for singing, dance, live music and other performances, with a flexible lineup based on available artists. Between acts, explore and progress your ofrenda; guides may offer informal activities. Set 3 unlocks on November 3 and set 4 on November 4; all five sets will be available by November 6.

04 /

Voices of Remembrance

Date to be confirmed · 7:00–10:00 PM EST

Share memories, family stories, legends and cultural tales in small groups around the gazebo and cemetery. A respectful space to remember and connect: tell a story or simply listen. All five sets will be available to finish your collectibles and personal ofrenda.

05 /

Night of Sharing & Community Connections

November 7 · 7:00–10:00 PM EST

An open, moderated evening without a major scheduled performance: meet people, invite friends and create spontaneous activities. Organizers will help you connect with Spanish-speaking members and VRChat communities. Collectibles, cultural locations and world progression systems will remain active.

06 /

Final Day & Closing Ceremony

Gathering · 7:00–10:00 PM EST · Closing around 10:00 PM EST

Finish your ofrenda, collect any missing items, revisit cultural locations and take photos with the community. A final path leads to a short show of music, lighting and particles, which may repeat approximately every 30 minutes. One of the main cycles will include our final community photo.

07 /

Daily Collectibles · The Cempasúchil Trail

November 1–5 · A new set each day

  • November 1: Veladora (candle)
  • November 2: Cempasúchil (marigold)
  • November 3: Pan de Muerto
  • November 4: Agua (water)
  • November 5: Papel Picado

Each set contains 3 matching objects hidden throughout the world, with every find saved automatically. Earlier sets remain available. Completing a set adds an element to your personal ofrenda and increases your contribution to The Flame of the Souls; finish all 5 sets, 15 collectibles in total, to complete your ofrenda.

08 /

Noche de las Ánimas · DJ Afterparties

Selected nights · From 10:00 PM EST

The celebration may continue with DJs after Journey to Mictlán, Performance Nights or Voices of Remembrance, depending on availability and the schedule. Not every night will have a DJ: check the corresponding event to confirm artists and times. The rest of the world stays open for exploring, socializing and finishing collectibles.
