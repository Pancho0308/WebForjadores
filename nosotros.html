Mission, Vision and Values

Mission

Position ourselves as a leading Spanish-speaking community in VRChat, being a living archive of knowledge in 2D art and 3D.

Vision

Be recognized internationally for our identity Latin American cultural culture and our collective projects in VR.

Values

Collaboration, respect, creativity and openness. a community where all Spanish speakers are welcome.
